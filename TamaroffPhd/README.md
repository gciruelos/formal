# P. Tamaroff, *Homotopical and effective methods for associative algebras*

PhD thesis, Trinity College Dublin, 2021 (advisors: Vladimir Dotsenko and Sergey Mozgovoy).
Source: [`TamaroffPhd.pdf`](TamaroffPhd.pdf). Build: `lake build TamaroffPhd` from the project root.

The thesis computes homotopy invariants of associative algebras through combinatorics of Anick
chains: explicit minimal models of monomial algebras, finite generation of Hochschild
cohomology for Gorenstein monomial algebras, and a homotopical proof of Bergman's Diamond Lemma.

## Main results

1. **Theorem 3.2.6.** A monomial algebra `k⟨X⟩/(W)` has a minimal model on its Anick chains,
   whose differential sums over all decompositions of a chain into smaller chains.
2. **Theorem 5.4.3.** A monomial algebra satisfies the finite generation conditions FG of
   Snashall–Solberg if and only if it is Gorenstein.
3. **Theorem 5.5.2.** The Hochschild cohomology of a Gorenstein monomial algebra is eventually
   periodic: cup product with an element `χ` of even degree is an isomorphism in high degrees.
4. **Theorem 6.3.2.** For every model of a monomial algebra extending its Shafarevich complex,
   a rewriting system is convergent iff the obstructions of the degree-two generators reduce to
   zero.
5. **Proposition 6.4.1 (Bergman's Diamond Lemma).** A rewriting system with well-founded
   ordering `<_Σ` (that is, a terminating one) is convergent iff all inclusion and overlap
   ambiguities are resolvable.

## The results in brief

An associative algebra is studied up to homotopy by replacing it with a *model*: a free graded
algebra `T(V)` with a differential whose homology is the algebra itself. Models compute
derived invariants such as Hochschild cohomology. They are hard to find in general. For
*monomial* algebras (quotients of a free algebra, or of a path algebra of a quiver, by words),
the thesis writes the minimal model down explicitly. Its generators are the Anick chains, the
words obtained by overlapping relations, and the differential of a chain sums over its
decompositions into smaller chains. Dually, this gives every higher product on the Yoneda algebra
`Ext_A(k, k)`. These products obey parity-based vanishing patterns, and the Chuang–King example
shows they can be non-trivial in infinitely many arities.

Chapter 5 applies these higher structures to support varieties. Snashall–Solberg's theory needs
Hochschild cohomology to satisfy finite generation conditions (FG), and FG forces the algebra
to be Gorenstein. For monomial algebras the converse holds. Over a Gorenstein monomial algebra,
high Anick chains are built by walking around *perfect cycles* (Chen–Shen–Zhou). Stable relation
cycles give *periodicity operators* `χ_γ` in the A∞-centre of the Yoneda algebra, and the
Yoneda algebra is a finite module over a polynomial ring `k[χ]` generated by them. As a
consequence, Hochschild cohomology becomes periodic past the Gorenstein dimension, and FG can be
decided by inspecting finitely many chains.

Chapter 6 recasts the Diamond Lemma homotopically. Convergence of a rewriting system means that
the algebra deforms the monomial algebra of its leading terms. Deformations of a model are
perturbations of its differential, that is solutions of the Maurer–Cartan equation in the
tangent complex. Only the generators of degree at most two matter, and the Maurer–Cartan
equation on a degree-two generator is exactly "this ambiguity is resolvable". Applied to the
inclusion–exclusion model this is Bergman's Diamond Lemma; applied to the minimal model it is
the Triangle Lemma.

Chapter 4 (Tamarkin–Tsygan calculus computed from models) is mostly operadic and is only
partly stated here.

This is a first-pass outline: definitions and statements, with `sorry` proofs, except
Proposition 6.4.1 (Bergman's Diamond Lemma), which is proved in full. The results the
thesis cites (Anick, Bardzell, Green–Happel–Zacharia, Chen–Shen–Zhou, Zaks, finitistic
dimensions, Erdmann–Holloway–Snashall–Solberg–Taillefer) are in the `Preliminaries*.lean`
files. Module docstrings list the statements that are not formalized yet and why.
